Refund Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

If Rightful isn't right for you, you can get your money back.

14-day refunds on the website

  • You can request a full refund within 14 days of any payment for a subscription bought on the website, whether it's your first payment or a renewal.
  • Email support@claimrightful.com from the email you signed in with, or reply to your receipt. You don't need to give a reason.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. Most arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Once refunded, the subscription is canceled and Premium access ends.

After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days aren't refundable, but you can cancel anytime in Profile → Cancel subscription. Your subscription won't renew, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Subscriptions bought on iPhone

Purchases made through the App Store are billed by Apple, and refunds follow Apple's policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Who processes refunds

Website orders are processed by our online resellers, Paddle.com or Gumroad, which act as the Merchant of Record for those orders. The one that processed your order issues approved refunds back to your payment method.
