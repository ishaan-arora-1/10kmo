Questions

Good questions to ask.

Is claiming settlement money legit?

Yes. When a company settles a class action, a court approves a fund and appoints an administrator to pay eligible people. Anyone who qualifies can file — you don't need a lawyer.

Does Rightful take a percentage of my payout?

No. You pay a flat subscription — billed securely through Razorpay. Every dollar the administrator sends is yours.

How much will I actually get?

It varies. Many payouts are $10–$100; some are larger. We show the estimated range from court documents, and final amounts depend on how many valid claims are filed.

Do I need receipts or proof?

Often not. Each settlement shows whether proof is required. Only file claims you truly qualify for — false claims can be illegal.

Can I use Rightful outside the US?

Rightful covers US class-action settlements. They're US court cases, so claims are open to people who used these companies while living in the United States, and administrators pay by US check or US bank transfer. If you lived in the US and have since moved abroad, you can usually still claim — check each settlement's official notice.

Is Rightful a law firm?

No. Rightful is an informational tool. It isn't a law firm, doesn't give legal advice, and isn't affiliated with any company, court, or settlement administrator.
