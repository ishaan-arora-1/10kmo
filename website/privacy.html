Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Rightful ("we", "us") makes an iPhone app and web app that help you find and track class-action settlements. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Companies you select. The apps and companies you tap so we can match you to settlements. Before you sign in, these stay on your device or in your browser.
  • Account details. If you sign in with Apple, Google, or an email link, we receive a user ID and, if you share them, your name and email address. With Apple's "Hide My Email," we only see a relay address.
  • Claim progress. Settlements you mark as filed, claim IDs you choose to enter, and payout amounts you record.
  • Subscription records. For iPhone purchases, Apple's signed transaction information; for web purchases, your Stripe customer and subscription status (such as plan and renewal date). We never see or store your full payment card.
  • Email reminders. If you turn them on, we use your account email to send deadline and new-match reminders. Turn them off anytime in Profile.
  • Browser storage. On the website, your selected companies and claim progress are saved in your browser until you sign in.
  • Notification token. If you turn on reminders, a device token Apple provides so we can send deadline and new-match alerts.

What we never collect

  • Bank, card, or email passwords, or access to your inbox or accounts.
  • The information you enter on official claim forms. Those forms are hosted by court-appointed settlement administrators and governed by their privacy policies.
  • Advertising identifiers. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

How we use it

Only to run Rightful: matching you to settlements, syncing your claims across devices, confirming your subscription, sending reminders you've turned on, and responding to support requests.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate the app: Supabase (secure database and sign-in hosting), Apple (Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, and push notifications), Google (Sign in with Google, if you choose it), Stripe (web payments), Resend (reminder and sign-in emails), and Vercel (website hosting). They process data only to provide those services.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated Rightful data at any time in the app under Profile → Delete account and data. Deleting your account cancels any web subscription. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your Apple ID settings.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database enforces per-user access so no one else can read your picks or claims.

Children

Rightful is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Use in-app deletion or email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@rightful.app
