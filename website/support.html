Support

We read every message and usually reply within one business day.

Email us support@rightful.app

Include the email you signed in with and, for a claim question, the settlement name.

Cancel or manage your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Rightful. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

Open Rightful, go to the subscription screen, and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

My claim was denied or hasn't paid

Rightful doesn't process claims — the court-appointed settlement administrator does. Use the claim ID you saved in Rightful to contact the administrator listed on the official settlement site. Payouts often take months after the final approval hearing.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your profile, company picks, and claim history. It does not cancel your Apple subscription.

Report a settlement error

Spotted a wrong deadline, payout, or link? Email us with the settlement name and the official notice link — we'll verify and fix it.
